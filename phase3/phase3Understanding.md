## What this project is

The long-term concept is camera-based inspection of one tyre from a low/front viewpoint, with separate appearance and geometric paths. The implemented research studies mileage-proxy classification, explanation diagnostics, tyre/tread localisation and six-point image-space boundary localisation. The implemented product is the native PySide6/PyTorch **Tread Station** in `prototype/`, despite the parent directory being named “web Applicarion.”

The workstation handles photos/videos, frozen-frame comparisons, overlapping region overlays, learned boundaries, evidence save/restore, image-space geometry, calibrated-target alignment and video export. It has already been built. Software checks do not establish independent field or physical measurement accuracy.

## Data and supervision

Original `final_v1` has **418 unique photographs** at native 1152×1536 resolution, **12 capture groups/user-confirmed distinct tyres**, and 4,180 augmented derivatives. The derivatives are not independent subjects. The original study uses its supplied three group folds; its 120-image geometry extension uses a different 72/24/24 split across 8/2/2 tyres.

The 418 manual indexed masks provide canonical tyre=`mask > 0` and tread=`mask in {2,3}`. Tyre and tread overlap. S5 used manual supervision; SAM2 agreement and blind consistency were deferred. The 120 geometry images have 720 visible boundary points at three fixed guide rows, not measured alignment or visibility-classifier labels.

Phase 2 adds **152 labelled frames** sampled at two targets/second from three videos (27/93/32 frames). These are three existing mid-mileage tyres, not three additional independent tyres. The total is **570 images**: 169 low, 249 mid, 152 high mileage-proxy labels. Exact video-to-original mid-tyre mapping remains unresolved.

The immutable source package intentionally has unassigned splits. Its separate runnable training overlay places all three original mid tyres and all video frames in training: **386 train / 81 validation / 103 test**, across 8/2/2 tyres. Held-out sets contain only old low/high tyres. Unknown mid-video mapping is therefore not a remaining training blocker for that completed protocol.

There are 45 new-frame tread/tyre containment disagreements. Source labels were retained: SegFormer ignores contradictory pixels; YOLO uses the documented training-only union policy and disables the incompatible exclusive-class auxiliary branch. HRNet uses polygon-derived new points as weak training targets with weight 0.25. Only old human points supply held-out point truth; Phase 2 HRNet test has 25 labelled photos.

## Limits that carry into future work

Mileage is a proxy, not measured tread depth or roadworthiness. More frames do not increase the independent tyre count. Phase 2 has no held-out mid/video cohort and no completed old-only A/B control, so it cannot establish a causal improvement from adding videos. Physical calibration/reference trials, broader tyre-disjoint field data, independent annotation agreement, and a justified healthy pool for PatchCore remain separate work.

The user's training, storage, HF publication and resume requirements are preserved in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Future training must deliver `.ipynb` notebooks and prove completed-update resume, while documenting the unpublished-work window after forced session death.

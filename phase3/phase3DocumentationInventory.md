## Coverage and interpretation

All files were loaded completely for a source/section scan and hashed. Primary operational documents received direct reading plus section review; long historical documents were reviewed through extracted section context. Identical copies were matched by hash, and differing manuscript build copies were compared with maintained source. This orientation is not a line-by-line scientific re-audit or exhaustive code review. The inventory records coverage honestly rather than treating every old instruction as current.

- **Direct + sections**: direct content inspection with section-context review; large combined outputs were supplemented with targeted reads/excerpts.
- **Section review**: full-source scan plus section-context review of historical/design/generated material.
- **Archive differences**: historical manuscript variant compared against maintained content.
- **Hash-identical copy**: same complete content as the linked reviewed path.

The complete hashes, file sizes, source timestamps and headings are in [markdown-baseline.json](audit/markdown-baseline.json); extracted context is in [markdown-section-review.json](audit/markdown-section-review.json).

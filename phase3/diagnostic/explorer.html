Interpretation and evidence

Uniform/oracle gates hold features and weights fixed. Oracle masks define diagnostic interventions and are unavailable in normal inference. Missing validation mid examples are not a measured mid-class result.

Training-set inference here is post-hoc nominal-image evaluation, not the unrecorded accuracy of augmented training batches. Tyres and clips remain dependent capture groups.

The gallery uses a fixed first image per split and tyre/clip for each checkpoint. Full per-image tables are saved separately. The application models and declared r1 export are unchanged.

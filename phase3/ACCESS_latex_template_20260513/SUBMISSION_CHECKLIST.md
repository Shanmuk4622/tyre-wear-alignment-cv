## Author actions before submission

| Priority | Decision | Review material / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Required | Confirm qualifying authors, order, affiliation, emails and biographies | Existing two-author line is a working line, not new approval |
| Required | All authors approve scientific content and exact submitted version | Complete PDF/source and claim ledger |
| Required | Confirm contributions / CRediT roles | Do not infer experimental, supervisory, writing or funding roles from names |
| Required | Confirm earlier manuscript's current status and relevant overlap | Same photos and prior components are shared. Resolve originality/disclosure before any simultaneous overlapping submission |
| Required | Declare funding, grants if any, competing interests | No grant, sponsor, conflict-free statement or endorsement fabricated |
| Required | Confirm image ownership and publication/redistribution permissions | Check release permission for the two identified atlas examples and both supplied portraits |
| Required | Decide public code/data scope and approve final availability statement | Existing HF fit/review public; new aggregation/native/paper derivatives local |
| Required | Review AI disclosure against final manuscript | Substantive drafting/code/figure-code assistance disclosed; no unassisted-writing claim |
| Required | Confirm applicable institutional ethics/data requirements | No approval or exemption asserted without a real determination |
| Portal | Submitting author provides public populated ORCID and declarations | No ORCID invented; no account accessed |
| Portal | Choose article type / topic keywords | Working suggestion: Research Article; negative outcomes remain explicit |
| Final | Recheck current rules, reference updates/retractions, source/PDF agreement | This task's source check is bounded, not a permanent retraction guarantee |

This records real-world decisions remaining after the local draft is complete.
No additional training or new photographs are prerequisites for author review.

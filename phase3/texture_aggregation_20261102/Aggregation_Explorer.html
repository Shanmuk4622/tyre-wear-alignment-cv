What the primary evidence says

  • One average retains about61% of local validation utility. A fixed position template plus five residual moments retains about82%.
  • Averaging disproportionately damages orientation and contrast detail. A fixed train template alone retains only about8% of the local advantage.
  • Full-context feature diversity narrows before pooling. Effective rank can also increase through invertible scaling, without new information.
  • High masked-context rank strongly tracks mask placement. The original projector uses unmasked full-context features.
